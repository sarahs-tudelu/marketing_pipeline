# Runway time-to-acceptable pilot — November 17, 2026

Actual paid generations against the current pipeline at commit 87d7c93. One canopy brief per configuration; these are individual observations, not reliable average latencies or success rates.

Nano Banana 2.1 was the fastest successful scene alternative. Standard Veo 3.1 was the fastest animation option when starting from the same approved still. The current configuration did not reach an acceptable scene within its normal retry limit. These results support a larger repeated trial of Nano Banana + Veo before changing production defaults.

## What acceptance means

The actual live gate must return all 19 checks passing for the finished Facebook video and caption: grounding, prices/offers validity, URLs, media URLs, length, hashtags, mentions/links, banned phrases, testimonials, disclosure, rights, media, video finish, provenance integrity, duplicates, audio review, vision review, model review and live readiness. No reviewer or gate is replaced with a fixture.

A passed result remains awaiting human approval. Nothing was scheduled or published. Video vision checks inspect the normal sampled frames, not every intervening frame; passing them is not proof of flawless motion.

## Measurement conditions and limits

- Time starts after source ingestion and job setup, before native prompt/reference work; it includes provider queues, uploads, generation, normal reviews/retries, music, assembly, caption and final checks. Failed runs have null time-to-acceptable, not their stopping duration.
- Normal retry limit: initial attempt plus up to three corrections. Same production defaults for review score (0.90), duplicate threshold (0.94), duplicate window (30 days), reviewer models and music model.
- All arms poll every five seconds for a controlled comparison. The production worker default is 15 seconds. Input download, cloud authentication, source snapshot and local database initialization are excluded.
- Trials used concurrent cohorts rather than fully randomized sequential scheduling. The matched animation cohort started together. Shared provider queue conditions and stochastic reviewers can affect results.
- Source inputs: three company-owned MODULUX C6SR00 grey product renders, the supplied Tudelu logo and approved facts dated November 16. Same brief/candidate pool; normal model-written scene prompts and reference selections may vary.
- The scene asks for a gray, solid-panel canopy at a brick office entrance, exactly two above-roof hanger rods with visible connections, plain glazing, no people, a locked camera and subtle foliage motion.
- Gen-4 uses a seed; the alternative image models do not accept one. Native portrait sizes differ: 720x1280, 768x1344, 768x1376 and 1088x1920. This compares usable settings, not equal pixel counts or deterministic draws.
- Duplicate checking used a read-only snapshot of 33 production posts. The API omits timestamps; imported posts were conservatively treated as recent. This can over-block old duplicates and is not a fresh pre-publication duplicate audit.
- Facebook video uses byte upload, so local-only media did not bypass a required public URL check. Instagram delivery was not tested.
- The shared conservative reservation ledger totals 1,139 of a maximum 1,800 credits. This is NOT actual billed cost and excludes reviewer APIs. Shared account balance changes cannot reliably measure this experiment's spend.
- An Ideogram repair costs more than one Gen-4 scene at the cited settings; its potential benefit is preserving a good scene and avoiding later failures. This pilot establishes that the repair worked, not that it is cheaper on average.

## Result to act on

Prioritize repeated tests of Nano Banana scenes with standard Veo animation across more canopy angles and other products. Retain masked repair as a conditional rescue for lettering-only failures. Three references did not help in this sample. Investigate the audio-review anomaly separately before drawing conclusions about Sunburst.

Production model defaults remain unchanged. The added CLI is an isolated benchmark harness, with bounded paid submissions, task/check evidence and rejection-preserving repair.
